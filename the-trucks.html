FAQ

Do I need a commercial licence?

Requirements depend on the vehicle class and your use case. Contact Candice and we’ll confirm what’s needed for your rental.

How much fits inside?

About 900 cu. ft. of cargo space, 120 sq. ft. of floor space, and up to 4,000 lb load capacity—ideal for delivery and moving routes.

What are the rental periods?

Flexible rental periods are available. Reach out to discuss short-term or longer commercial needs.

Where do you serve?

We’re based in Orangeville and support operators across Owen Sound, Barrie, and the Greater Toronto Area.

Is there curbside access?

Yes. Curbside entry helps crews load and unload more safely without walking through traffic.

Is there a ramp?

Answer to follow.

What about fuel?

Return the truck with the same fuel level as when you picked it up. If additional fuel is required after return, fuel charges will apply.

How clean should the truck be on return?

Trucks should be returned in reasonably clean condition. Excessive cleaning may result in additional cleaning charges.
